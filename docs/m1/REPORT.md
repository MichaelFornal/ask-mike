- On requirements from job descriptions, R4 reaches recall@10 of 0.54 (0.52 to 0.56),

## What was measured

Ask Mike will answer questions about Michael Fornal from a public corpus of 400 evidence units
(`corpus/units.jsonl`). Before it can answer, it has to find the right units. M1 measures that step alone: given
one requirement from a job description, or one recruiter question, how many of the relevant units come back
near the top of the list.

The 5 rungs compared:

| Rung | Method |
|---|---|
| R1 | BM25 keyword search (SQLite FTS5) |
| R2 | Embedding search with bge-small-en-v1.5 (sqlite-vec) |
| R3 | R1 and R2 merged by reciprocal rank fusion (RRF) |
| R4 | R3, with the BM25 query widened by the skill aliases in `corpus/skills.json` |
| KW | Keyword-match baseline: units ranked by how many of the query's words and named skills they contain |

Relevance is binary. The metrics are recall@5, recall@10, MRR@10 and nDCG@10. A fixed hash puts each job
description and each question in a tune split or a report split. Settings were chosen on the tune split; every
retrieval metric below is from the report split. Each number is a mean with a 95% interval from a
cluster bootstrap (2,000 resamples) that resamples whole job descriptions or whole questions.

## Sets and sizes

Sizes here count both splits. The results below give the report-split counts.

| Set | Size | Relevance labels |
|---|---|---|
| Job descriptions | 100 real postings, split into 1,446 requirements | AI-graded |
| Recruiter questions | 42 questions about Mike's work | AI-graded |
| Personal questions | 16 questions on work authorization, location and start date | fixed gold units |

claude-sonnet-5-5 split each job description into requirements,
at most 15 per job description. The job descriptions, requirements and recruiter questions
stay private; only these aggregates are published.
Of the 16 personal questions, 12 are invented phrasings
written for this set.

For each requirement and question, a pool was formed from the top results of every rung under every tuning
setting. Judge A (claude-opus-5-5) marked each pooled unit relevant or not. Under judge A,
90% of report-split requirements have at least one relevant unit. For the rest, no
pooled unit was judged relevant; they are counted but left out of the means. judged@10 is the share of a rung's
top-10 results that sit in the judged pool. The lowest judged@10 of any report-split query, under any rung, is
1.00. Report-split queries without a label row: 0.

## Grader agreement

Labels are AI-graded. Judge A (claude-opus-5-5) graded every AI-graded pool. Judge B (claude-sonnet-5-5)
graded a hash-picked sample on its own: 20 report-split job descriptions and
all 42 recruiter questions.

- Cohen's κ (agreement corrected for chance) over the 9,167 (query, unit) pairs both
  judges graded, across both splits: 0.60.
- Kendall's τ between the rung orders by recall@10 under judge A and under judge B, on the
  293 report-split requirements both judges labelled: 1.00. Ties
  in a rung order break by rung name, ascending.

No human labelled a sample to check either judge. That was Mike's choice.

## Tuned settings

R3 and R4 fuse the top 50 results of BM25 and of embedding search, with RRF constant
k0 = 10. The pair with the highest R4 recall@10 on the tune split, under judge A, was
chosen; the rule and the grid are in `askmike/retrieval/settings.json`.

## Limitations

- Labels are AI-graded: judge A is claude-opus-5-5 on every AI-graded pool, judge B is claude-sonnet-5-5 on a
  sample. There is no human-labelled check; that was Mike's choice.
- Judge B's labels feed only the rung-order check (τ) and the agreement score (κ); every retrieval metric value
  comes from judge A's labels, except the personal questions, which use fixed gold units.
- Recall is relative to the judged pool. A relevant unit that no rung and no tuning setting retrieved was never
  graded, so it counts against no rung (pooling bias). recall@k cannot reach a perfect score for requirements
  with more than k relevant units.
- Requirements were extracted by claude-sonnet-5-5 and capped at 15 per job description;
  82% of job descriptions reached the cap, so any requirements beyond it were not
  measured. Extraction errors carry into every number.
- R4's alias expansion matches skill names of 3 characters or fewer (such as SQL
  or AWS) only when they are capitalised or upper case, as they usually are in the extracted requirements;
  lowercase short names in typed questions are not expanded. M2 will revisit this.
- τ over 5 rungs moves in steps of 0.2.
- One corpus snapshot: every number is for the 400 units exported in M0. A new export changes
  them.
- The recruiter-question and personal sets are small, so their intervals are wide.
